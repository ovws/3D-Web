# Page topology

1. Full-viewport WebGPU/WebGL canvas with an isometric chase camera.
2. Intro spawn ring and contextual control label.
3. Driveable low-poly off-road vehicle with physics, jump, boost, brake, horn, hydraulics, respawn, and camera orbit.
4. Continuous explorable world containing landing, project, career, social, laboratory, circuit, achievement, and hidden areas.
5. Fixed menu and map triggers above the canvas.
6. Full-screen menu with home, options, controls, achievements, circuit, whispers, and behind-the-scenes panels.
7. Touch controls and gamepad-aware control labels on supported devices.
8. Modal and notification layers for interactive points and progression.

The experience is spatial rather than scroll-driven. Movement through the world is the navigation model.
